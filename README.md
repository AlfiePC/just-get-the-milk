# Just Get the Milk

A sensory awareness experience from Prior's Court. Walk a supermarket, find three items and pay, while a randomly dealt sensory profile makes the environment hostile.

- `index.html` – desktop / phone version
- `vr.html` – Meta Quest (WebXR) version: open in the Quest browser and press Enter VR

Headphones recommended. Contains layered sound, pulsing light (capped at 2 flashes per second), blur and camera movement. Not for anyone with photosensitive epilepsy, migraine, motion sickness or their own sensory sensitivities.
